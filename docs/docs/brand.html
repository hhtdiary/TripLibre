Logo & brand

The TripLibre app icon, website and docs all use one logo. This page shows the current version, how to use it, and the earlier versions it replaced.

Current logo

A bold white T on a sky-blue background, with the travel modes TripLibre detects around it: plane, bus, rail, walking, cycling and car. The road starts at the foot of the T and winds out of the frame.

Using the logo

  • Keep it square and opaque. Don't add rounded corners or a border: iOS and most platforms apply their own mask.
  • Keep the whole T and its symbols in frame. Don't crop, recolour or stretch it.
  • The logo identifies the TripLibre app and its docs. HHT Diary, the organization, has its own mark.
