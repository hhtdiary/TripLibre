What TripLibre is

TripLibre is a free, open-source iOS app that records where you go, rebuilds each day into visits, trips and travel modes, and keeps all of it on your phone. Think of it as a household travel survey that fills itself in, with a sample size of one. It has no account and no server, and exports to CSV, JSON, GeoJSON and SQLite.

TripLibre was called HHT until October 2026. It is the first product of HHT Diary, the organization behind this site. The app still shows as HHT on your Home Screen and in Settings, and the code keeps HHT in its names (HHTCore, HHT.xcodeproj, analysis/hht.py).

i

TripLibre is not on the App Store yet. You build it from source with Xcode and install it on your own phone. With a free Apple ID the install lasts 7 days and you renew it with one command, and your data is kept between installs. See Installation.
