Who we are

Travel data that people can see, check and own.

Where we go says a great deal about our lives, yet the tools that record it usually keep the data on someone else's server, and the tools researchers use to study travel are expensive and closed. HHT Diary is an open-source effort to make travel-behaviour data collection more accessible, transparent, affordable and user-controlled, for everyday people and for the researchers who plan our cities.

Free

No subscription, paid tier, ads or data resale.

Local-first

Data stays on the person's device until they choose to export it.

Transparent

Open code, documented rules, and accuracy measured in public.

Honest

Every page separates what exists today from what is only planned.

What we build

Products, and the open engine they share.

The organization sets the principles and the shared data model; each product is something you install and run. There is one product today.

Product

TripLibre

A free, open-source travel diary for iPhone. It rebuilds each day into visits, trips and modes, keeps the data on the phone, and exports it for analysis.

Available · build from source

Visit TripLibre →

Shared foundation

HHTCore

The Swift package behind TripLibre: collection, inference, corrections and export. Any future HHT Diary tool would reuse it, so that data from all of them can be analysed the same way.
