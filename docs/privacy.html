Concepts

Privacy

HHT has no account, no server, no analytics SDK and no sync. Your travel history stays on your iPhone unless you export it yourself.

What is stored

One SQLite file inside the app's sandbox (Library/Application Support/HHT/travel.sqlite) holds raw GPS fixes, CoreMotion activity, inferred and corrected visits and trips, your places, flights, life phases, stamps you tapped, and the audit log. A stamp keeps only a coarse location (rounded to about 110 m) and doesn't need a precise fix to be saved.

  • Daily snapshots go to Documents/Backups (the last 7 are kept).
  • Exports go to Documents/Exports.
  • For the Home Screen widgets, a small summary file (widget-snapshot.json) sits in the app's App Group container. It holds counts, times and the names of places you named yourself: no GPS points, routes, coordinates or addresses.
  • Both are visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › HHT.

Your iOS device backup (iCloud or Finder) includes the app's container, like any other app's data. Turn off iCloud Backup for HHT if you don't want that.

You're in control

  • Stop collection any time: Settings › Record location.
  • Export everything at any time as CSV, JSON, GeoJSON or SQLite.
  • Erase all data permanently deletes the database contents (two confirmations). Backups and exports in Files are left for you to delete yourself.
  • Soft-deleted records stay in the database for the audit trail until you erase.

Sharing safely

Raw traces and daily diaries reveal where you live, work and sleep. If you publish anything, share aggregates only: cities, airports, flight routes, mode shares. Never share raw_location_points, visits, or trip routes near home.

Verifying it yourself

Because HHT is open source, you don't have to take this page's word for it. All network access is in three places:

  • App/Location/PlaceGeocoder.swift: optional reverse geocoding
  • The airport-list download in App/Screens/SettingsView.swift
  • MapKit views (map tiles)
